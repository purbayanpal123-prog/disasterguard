SECTION 01

The Real Crisis: Why Current Relief Fails

Every monsoon in Assam (Brahmaputra Basin), Bengal, Bihar and Kerala, flash floods claim hundreds of lives.

📞 ❌

Emergency Lines Jammed

During cyclones or flash floods, 112, 100, and 108 phone lines receive thousands of concurrent calls. The golden rescue hour is lost in call waiting.

Result: 60%+ Calls Dropped
🌊 🚜

Blind Rescue Dispatches

Rescue squads (NDRF / SDRF) enter disaster zones without knowing which roads are underwater, resulting in boats and ambulances getting trapped in flash floods.

Result: Rescue Teams Stranded
📍 ❓

Zero Spatial Location

Panicking victims cannot give accurate landmarks in flooded darkness. Rescuers search blindly over massive square kilometer zones without exact GPS pins.

Result: Lethal Delays in Rescue
⚠️ Over 75% of flood casualties happen due to delayed response and lack of coordinated geographic data. DisasterGuard Fixes This
